# 🏎️ Restoration Case Study: Kyosho DBX 1/10 Nitro

## 📋 Executive Summary
This project documents the **corrective maintenance and operational recovery** of a Kyosho DBX 1/10 Nitro buggy (GXR-18 engine) following a 10-year dormancy period.

The objective is to apply **Agile/Kanban Project Management** methodologies and **Mechanical Engineering** principles to restore full functionality. The project operates under strict budget and time constraints, aimed at liquidating the asset in the secondary market.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives (OKRs)

### 🛠️ Technical Goals
* **Engine Revival:** Achieve stable ignition and idle cycles for the GXR-18 engine without requiring a rebuild or assembly replacement.
* **Control System Validation:** Restore full responsiveness to the transmitter commands (Steering, Throttle acceleration, and Braking).
* **Operational Status:** Return the vehicle to a fully functional "Run-Ready" state.

### 💰 Financial & Management Goals
* **Budget Efficiency:** Keep total Cost of Repair (CAPEX) below 15% of the estimated asset market value (~R$ 800.00).
* **Documentation:** Record the entire diagnostic and execution lifecycle using GitHub Projects.

---

## ⚙️ Methodology & Strategy

### The "Surgical" Approach (MVP)
Due to the prolonged storage time, a full "nut-and-bolt" overhaul was ruled out to avoid catastrophic failure of aged polymers (plastic parts). The scope was strictly defined to focus only on critical systems (Minimum Viable Product strategy):
* **Power Unit:** Engine block and Exhaust system.
* **Fuel System:** Fuel tank, carburetor, and supply lines.
* **Electronics:** Transmitter (TX), Receiver (RX), and Servos.

## 🛠️ Key Engineering Challenges

### 1. Engine Seizure (Piston Lock)
* **Diagnosis:** The piston is seized to the cylinder sleeve due to the polymerization of old castor oil (fuel residue).
* **Proposed Solution:** Application of penetrating solvent combined with **induced thermal expansion** (heating the aluminum block) to release the piston without damaging the connecting rod.

### 2. Fuel System Degradation
* **Diagnosis:** Varnish deposits inside the carburetor and loss of elasticity in silicone lines.
* **Proposed Solution:** Chemical flush of the tank and complete replacement of fuel lines.
